# 1.0.0 - Initial Release

- **Product Management**: CRUD for products with stock tracking.
- **AI-Powered Descriptions**: Generate marketing-style product descriptions.
- **Purchases Module**: Record procurement entries and update stock.
- **Sales Module**: Create invoices, generate PDFs, and update stock.
- **Customer Ledger**: Track customer dues and payments.
- **Dashboard & Reports**: Summaries of sales, purchases, profit, and dues.
- **Voice-Based Search**: Find products using voice commands.
- **Security & Settings**: Dark mode and PIN lock.
- **Barcode Scanning**: Scan barcodes to add products to invoices.
- **Expense Tracking**: Record and manage business expenses.
- **Backup & Restore**: Backup and restore data to a zip file.

## Next Phase Roadmap

- **Stock Alerts**: Notifications for low stock levels.
- **Processing Module**: Convert raw materials into finished products.
- **Finance & Reports**: More detailed financial reports.
- **Smart Insights (AI)**: AI-powered business insights.
- **Multi-User Roles**: Admin, Manager, and Cashier roles.
- **Activity Logs**: Track user actions.
- **Multi-language Support**: English and Hindi localization.
- **Cloud Backup (Optional)**: Firebase/Supabase integration.
- **App Update Notifier**: Notify users of new app versions.
